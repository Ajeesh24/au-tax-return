# Drop your tax documents here

Put the files for your tax return **in this folder**, then tell Claude to start
(e.g. *"I've added my documents — help me do my 2025–26 return"*). Claude will read
everything in here, classify each file, and pull out the figures it needs.

## What to drop in

Anything you have — PDFs or photos/screenshots are fine:

- **PAYG income statement / payment summary** (from myGov or your employer)
- **Bank / broker interest & tax summary** (interest earned, and interest charged on loans)
- **Loan / mortgage statements** (interest, borrowing costs)
- **Rental managing-agent annual statement** (the best single source if you have one)
- **Invoices & receipts** for property expenses (repairs, cleaning, pest, insurance, council, water, agent fees, safety checks…)
- **Depreciation schedule** (from a quantity surveyor)
- **Property settlement statement** (if you bought or sold)
- **Contract notes / share or crypto records** (for capital gains)

Tips:
- Clear photos are fine, but readable PDFs are best. If a scan is blurry, Claude will
  ask you to type the key numbers.
- One file per document is easiest, but multi-page PDFs are fine.
- You don't need to rename or sort them — just drop them in.

## Privacy — important

⚠️ **Files you place here are ignored by git and are NOT committed or shared.** They
stay on your machine. The repository's `.gitignore` excludes everything in this folder
except this README. Never commit personal tax documents.

When you're done with your return, you can delete the files from this folder.
